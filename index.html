Chronicle

World model season-0.5: rigid plates on 100,002 geodesic cells drive relief; a seasonal zonal energy-balance model with ocean mixed-layer and land heat capacity (so seasons are damped by the sea and strongest deep inland), a prescribed three-cell wind pattern that follows the seasons, and moisture transport across the mesh produce temperature, rain shadows, Köppen climates and Whittaker-style biomes. Runoff from a Budyko water balance drains over the terrain into rivers, lakes and drainage basins; lakes that evaporate more than they receive become salt lakes with closed basins. Conceptual approximations, not a validated Earth-system model.
